Darshan's Eight Goals: How Legacy FFA's Number 7 Took The League Lead

Knuckleball Editorial · 12 November 2026

Six against NBFC, two against United 5's. Darshan has scored two thirds of Legacy FFA's goals and opened a four-goal gap at the top of the charts.

Short answer: Darshan has scored eight goals in two Baller League matches for Legacy FFA (six in a 7-1 win over NBFC and two in a 5-1 win over United 5's), making him the outright leading scorer in Season 02 so far.

Two games, two wins, one obvious threat

Legacy FFA opened their Group B campaign with a 7-1 win over NBFC and followed it with a 5-1 win over United 5's. The two results put them top of the group on six points with a goal difference of +10, level on points with Passion FC but six goals better off.

Across those two matches Legacy FFA scored 12 goals. Darshan scored eight of them, two thirds of his team's entire output, and no other player in either group has more than four.

Six goals against NBFC

The opening fixture set the tone for Legacy FFA. They beat NBFC 7-1, and Darshan scored six. Vishwa added the seventh, while Avinash scored NBFC's consolation. It remains the biggest individual haul of the season and the largest margin of victory in Group B.

Two more against United 5's

United 5's had won their opener 4-1 against Falcons FC, so it was a meeting of two sides who had won their opening match. Darshan scored twice, Vky scored twice and Trippzy added one in a 5-1 win. Ankit scored United 5's goal.

The brace mattered for the standings as much as the record books: with Passion FC also winning twice, Legacy FFA needed the goal difference, and their +10 is now the best in Group B.

The scoring chart after Matchday 1

  • Darshan (Legacy FFA): 8 goals, 2 matches
  • Adnan (Bangalore Grafters FC): 4 goals, 2 matches
  • Dilsher (Passion FC): 4 goals, 2 matches
  • Fazil (Bangalore Grafters FC): 3 goals
  • Sanjay (Crescents FC): 3 goals
  • Muied (First Touch FC): 3 goals
  • Shree (Walkalone FC): 3 goals

Why eight goals matter in a group stage

Group tables are decided on points first and goal difference next, and both groups already have teams level on points. Legacy FFA and Passion FC are tied on six points in Group B; United 4th Phase lead Group A with six, with three teams on three behind them.

In that situation, a striker who turns tight wins into four-goal margins changes the table without changing the win count. Darshan's eight goals have given Legacy FFA a +10 goal difference that no other Group B side is close to: Passion FC are +4, First Touch FC +2.

Where to find Darshan's season record

Darshan wears number 7 for Legacy FFA. His goals, appearances and honours are tracked on his player profile, and his squad card sits in the Legacy FFA section of the players page. The full scorer list and both tables are on the Competition page.

What comes next

Legacy FFA are back on Sunday 15 November with two Group B fixtures: Falcons FC at 12:00 PM and Crescents FC at 1:00 PM, both on Turf 1. Group B's Matchday 1 results and scorers are already live in the competition data.
