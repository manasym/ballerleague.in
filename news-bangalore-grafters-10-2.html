Bangalore Grafters 10-2 Walkalone: Adnan Hits Four In Group A Goal Rush

Knuckleball Editorial · 9 November 2026

Adnan scored four and Fazil added three as Bangalore Grafters produced the biggest scoreline of Group A so far.

Bangalore Grafters FC responded to their opening 2-1 defeat with a 10-2 win over Walkalone FC — the biggest scoreline in Group A so far. Adnan scored four, Fazil hit a hat-trick, and three other Grafters players got on the scoresheet.

The scorers

  • Bangalore Grafters FC: Adnan 4, Fazil 3, Abhinav 1, Aju 1, Mathai 1
  • Walkalone FC: Shree 2

A response after the opening loss

Grafters lost 2-1 to United 4th Phase in their first match. The 10-2 win was a complete turnaround and moved them to second in Group A on goal difference. Aju, who scored in the first match, was also on target in the second.

The table after the result

  • Bangalore Grafters FC — 3 pts from 2 games, GD +7, second
  • Walkalone FC — 0 pts from 2 games, GD -10, sixth
  • United 4th Phase — 6 pts, first

Adnan leads the scoring race

Adnan's four goals make him the early top scorer in Group A. Fazil's three goals put him second, ahead of Chethan, Daniel, Shree and AD on two each.

Why the result matters

In a group where goal difference could decide the top two, an eight-goal margin is significant. Grafters' +7 goal difference is the best in Group A after Matchday 1.

What's next
