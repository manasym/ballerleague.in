United 5's 4-1 Falcons FC: Yashwanth's Double And An Own Goal

Knuckleball Editorial · 12 November 2026

Three United 5's players found the net and a fourth goal came off their own defender, leaving Falcons FC still searching for a first point.

Short answer: United 5's beat Falcons FC 4-1 in Group B, with Yashwanth scoring twice and Ankit and Nitesh adding one each. Falcons' only goal came from an own goal by United 5's player Swapnil.

The scorers

  • United 5's: Yashwanth 2, Ankit 1, Nitesh 1
  • Falcons FC: Own goal (Swapnil, United 5's)

Three scorers and an own goal

United 5's spread their goals across three players, but the match also produced an own goal from their own defender Swapnil, which counted for Falcons FC. It was the only goal Falcons managed in the match, and the only goal they have scored in Group B so far.

Yashwanth's brace is his first multi-goal return of the season and puts him on two goals, level with team-mate Ankit, who scored in this match and again in the 5-1 defeat to Legacy FFA.

Falcons FC's tough start

Falcons FC have now lost both Group B matches. They went down 2-1 to NBFC, with Alvin jr scoring, before this 4-1 defeat. Their goal difference of -4 leaves them eighth, level on points with Veltrix United but behind on goals scored (2 to 3).

Where the result leaves both teams

  • United 5's: 4th in Group B, 3 pts, GD -1
  • Falcons FC: 8th in Group B, 0 pts, GD -4

A win that keeps United 5's in the pack

Four Group B teams are level on three points. First Touch FC lead that quartet on +2, with United 5's next on -1 ahead of Crescents FC (-2) and NBFC (-5). A second win would move United 5's level with the top two on six.

Do own goals count in the scoring chart?

No. Own goals are recorded in the match result and in the scoreline, but they are not credited to a player in the league's top-scorers table. Falcons' goal against United 5's is listed as an own goal, which is why no Falcons player appears on the scoresheet for that match.

What's next

Both clubs are back on Sunday 15 November. United 5's play NBFC at 12:30 PM on Turf 1 and First Touch FC at 1:30 PM on Turf 2; Falcons FC play Legacy FFA at 12:00 PM on Turf 1 and Veltrix United at 1:00 PM on Turf 2.
