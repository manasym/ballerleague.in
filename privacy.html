Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who we are

The Baller League is operated by Knuckleball Global Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru, India.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details you submit: name, email, phone, team or player name, match details and links.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location, if analytics is enabled.

Why we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and deliver content or merchandise you ask for.
  • To publish league results, fixtures and player statistics.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure site traffic. Google may set cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with providers that host the site, process email or provide analytics, and where the law requires it.

Retention

Enquiry emails are kept for up to 24 months. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data by emailing hello@theballerleague.in.

Contact

Knuckleball Global Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Email: hello@theballerleague.in.
