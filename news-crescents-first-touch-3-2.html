Crescents FC 3-2 First Touch FC: Sanjay's Double Settles A Five-Goal Game

Knuckleball Editorial · 12 November 2026

Sanjay's second goal of the match gave Crescents FC a win that lifts them above First Touch FC's goal difference in a crowded Group B middle.

Short answer: Crescents FC beat First Touch FC 3-2 in Group B, with Sanjay scoring twice and Manoj once. Muied scored both of First Touch's goals.

The scorers

  • Crescents FC: Sanjay 2, Manoj 1
  • First Touch FC: Muied 2

Sanjay's double decides a five-goal game

First Touch FC had beaten Veltrix United 3-0 in their opener; Crescents FC had lost 4-1 to Passion FC. The win left both clubs on three points, with First Touch FC still ahead on goal difference.

Sanjay's two goals took him to three for the season, level with Muied and Shree and behind Darshan (8) and the pair on four, Adnan and Dilsher. Manoj's goal was his first of the campaign.

Muied's brace for First Touch FC

Muied scored both First Touch goals, adding to the one he scored in the 3-0 win over Veltrix United. His three goals make him First Touch FC's leading scorer and one of seven players in the league with three or more.

Where the result leaves both teams

  • First Touch FC: 3rd in Group B, 3 pts, GD +2
  • Crescents FC: 5th in Group B, 3 pts, GD -2

A crowded middle of Group B

Four teams (First Touch FC, United 5's, Crescents FC and NBFC) are level on three points. First Touch FC's +2 goal difference is the best of that quartet, which keeps them third despite the defeat.

The players involved

Sanjay and Manoj appear in the Crescents FC squad, and Muied in the First Touch FC squad. Individual season records, including goals and appearances, are on each player profile.

What's next

Crescents FC are back on Sunday 15 November against Veltrix United at 12:00 PM on Turf 2 and Legacy FFA at 1:00 PM on Turf 1. All results, scorer lines and the live table are on the Competition page.
