Passion FC Start 2-0: Dilsher's Hat-Trick Seals A 4-3 Thriller Against Veltrix

Knuckleball Editorial · 12 November 2026

Two wins, eight goals and a hat-trick from Dilsher. Passion FC are level on points with Legacy FFA in Group B and four goals behind on goal difference.

Short answer: Passion FC have won both Group B matches, beating Crescents FC 4-1 and Veltrix United 4-3. Dilsher scored a hat-trick in the second game and has four goals for the season; Wani has two.

Passion FC 4-1 Crescents FC

Passion FC's opener was a shared effort: Dhruv, Shreyas, Dilsher and Wani scored one each, with Sanjay replying for Crescents FC. It was the first of two matches in which Passion FC scored four.

Passion FC 4-3 Veltrix United

Veltrix United made the second fixture the closest game of the group. Maaziee scored twice and Chetan once, but Dilsher's hat-trick and a second goal of the round for Wani gave Passion FC a 4-3 win.

  • Passion FC: Dilsher 3, Wani 1
  • Veltrix United: Maaziee 2, Chetan 1

Dilsher's four goals in two games

Dilsher opened his account in the 4-1 win over Crescents and then scored three of Passion FC's four against Veltrix. His four goals put him level with Adnan of Bangalore Grafters FC as the joint second-highest scorer in the league, behind only Darshan's eight.

His season record is on his player profile, alongside the rest of the Passion FC squad on the players page.

The other side of a perfect start

Passion FC have conceded four goals in two matches, one against Crescents and three against Veltrix. Of the two teams on six points in Group B, Legacy FFA have conceded two and scored 12 to Passion's eight. Together those add up to the six-goal gap in goal difference: Legacy FFA are +10, Passion FC +4.

Where Passion FC sit

  • Legacy FFA: 6 pts, GD +10
  • Passion FC: 6 pts, GD +4
  • First Touch FC: 3 pts, GD +2
  • United 5's: 3 pts, GD -1
  • Crescents FC: 3 pts, GD -2

The names on the scoresheet

Four different Passion FC players have scored in Group B: Dilsher (4), Wani (2), Dhruv (1) and Shreyas (1). Spreading goals across the squad is a useful buffer in a group where goal difference is already separating the top two.

Next for Passion FC

Passion FC are back on Sunday 15 November against First Touch FC at 12:30 PM on Turf 2 and NBFC at 1:30 PM on Turf 1. Their two results and every scorer line are already published on the Competition page, and the full Group B round is covered in the Matchday 1 recap.
