WLWG FC 4-2 Walkalone FC: The 2-0 Comeback That Shook Group A

Knuckleball Editorial · 9 November 2026

Walkalone led 2-0. WLWG scored four. Here is how the comeback changed the early Group A picture in Bangalore's best amateur league.

WLWG FC looked down and out at 2-0. By full time, they had won 4-2. The comeback was the standout result of Group A's opening matchday and one of the reasons the Baller League is drawing attention as Bangalore's best amateur football league.

The scoreline

WLWG FC 4-2 Walkalone FC. Walkalone led 2-0 through Shree and Rohan. WLWG then scored four: Daniel twice, Rishabh once and Puneeth once. The result gave WLWG three points from two games.

The comeback, in context

The game was decided by WLWG's response after conceding the first two goals. Three different players contributed to the turnaround, with Daniel leading the way on two goals. Walkalone, who scored twice through Shree and Rohan, could not hold the lead.

A 2-0 advantage is often enough in amateur football. WLWG's four-goal response shows the value of squad depth and composure under pressure.

What it does to Group A

  • WLWG FC: 3 pts from 2 games, GD 0, fourth
  • Walkalone FC: 0 pts from 2 games, GD -10, sixth
  • United 4th Phase: 6 pts, first
  • Bangalore Grafters FC, NEXUS FC and WLWG FC: 3 pts each

Why it matters

In a group where four teams are separated by three points, the comeback could be worth more than three points by the end of the season. It also keeps WLWG within touching distance of the top two before the next round of fixtures.

Top scorers after Matchday 1

Adnan of Bangalore Grafters FC leads the Group A scoring list with four goals. Fazil, also of Bangalore Grafters FC, has three. Daniel's brace puts him level with Chethan, Shree and AD on two goals.
