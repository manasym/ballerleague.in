Group B Matchday 1 Recap: Eight Results, 42 Goals And Legacy FFA On Top

Knuckleball Editorial · 12 November 2026

Legacy FFA and Passion FC both made it two wins from two, Darshan scored eight goals in two games, and Group B produced 42 goals in eight matches.

Short answer: Legacy FFA lead Group B on goal difference after two wins, Passion FC are the only other team with a perfect record, and Darshan of Legacy FFA tops the league scoring chart with eight goals. Group B's eight Matchday 1 fixtures produced 42 goals, the biggest win being Legacy FFA's 7-1 defeat of NBFC.

Every Group B result

Group B played eight matches on Sunday 1 November at Play365 Vidyaranyapura, Bengaluru. The eight fixtures followed Group A's five, and every one produced at least three goals.

  • United 5's 4-1 Falcons FC: Yashwanth 2, Ankit, Nitesh; Falcons' goal was an own goal
  • Legacy FFA 7-1 NBFC: Darshan 6, Vishwa; Avinash for NBFC
  • Passion FC 4-1 Crescents FC: Dhruv, Shreyas, Dilsher, Wani; Sanjay for Crescents
  • First Touch FC 3-0 Veltrix United: Danish 2, Muied
  • NBFC 2-1 Falcons FC: Sai, Sam; Alvin jr for Falcons
  • Legacy FFA 5-1 United 5's: Darshan 2, Vky 2, Trippzy; Ankit for United 5's
  • Crescents FC 3-2 First Touch FC: Sanjay 2, Manoj; Muied 2 for First Touch
  • Passion FC 4-3 Veltrix United: Dilsher 3, Wani; Maaziee 2, Chetan for Veltrix

Who scored in Group B on Matchday 1?

Darshan's six goals against NBFC are the single biggest haul of the round in either group. Four players scored three or more across the eight matches: Darshan (8 in total), Dilsher (4), Sanjay (3) and Muied (3).

  • 8 goals: Darshan (Legacy FFA)
  • 4 goals: Dilsher (Passion FC)
  • 3 goals: Sanjay (Crescents FC), Muied (First Touch FC)
  • 2 goals: Maaziee (Veltrix United), Wani (Passion FC), Vky (Legacy FFA), Danish (First Touch FC), Yashwanth (United 5's), Ankit (United 5's)

The Group B table after Matchday 1

Group B is separated by goal difference at the top: Legacy FFA and Passion FC both have six points from two matches, with Legacy's +10 the best defensive-and-attacking balance in the group.

  • Legacy FFA: 2 played, 2 won, GD +10, 6 pts
  • Passion FC: 2 played, 2 won, GD +4, 6 pts
  • First Touch FC: 2 played, 1 won, GD +2, 3 pts
  • United 5's: 2 played, 1 won, GD -1, 3 pts
  • Crescents FC: 2 played, 1 won, GD -2, 3 pts
  • NBFC: 2 played, 1 won, GD -5, 3 pts
  • Veltrix United: 2 played, 0 won, GD -4, 0 pts
  • Falcons FC: 2 played, 0 won, GD -4, 0 pts

Five numbers that explain Group B

  • 42 goals in eight matches: an average of 5.25 per game, and 14 more goals than Group A managed in five.
  • 12 goals for Legacy FFA: the most of any team in the group, and ten more than Falcons FC managed.
  • One clean sheet: First Touch FC's 3-0 win over Veltrix United was the only shutout in Group B.
  • Four teams on three points: First Touch, United 5's, Crescents and NBFC all sit within one result of each other in mid-table.
  • One own goal in the round: United 5's defender Swapnil turned one in for Falcons, and it was the only goal Falcons scored.

Group A vs Group B after Matchday 1

Group A has produced 28 goals in five matches, with Bangalore Grafters FC's 10-2 win over Walkalone FC the standout scoreline. Group B's 42 goals came in three more matches, and it has the round's leading scorer: Darshan's eight goals are double the Group A best of four, set by Adnan of Bangalore Grafters FC.

The combined top-scorers list is published on the Competition page, where every result, scorer line and both group tables are kept up to date. Individual tallies also appear on each player card and on the player profiles.

What happens next in Group B

Group B's Matchday 2 fixtures are on Sunday 15 November, starting at 12:00 PM on Turf 1 with Falcons FC vs Legacy FFA. The eight Matchday 1 results are the only completed Group B matches in the competition data, and every scorer listed above is already reflected in the league's top-scorers table.

Frequently asked questions

Who is top of Group B in the Baller League?

Legacy FFA are top of Group B with six points from two matches and a goal difference of +10. Passion FC are second on six points with +4.

Who is the top scorer in Group B?

Darshan of Legacy FFA has eight goals from two matches, six against NBFC and two against United 5's. Dilsher of Passion FC is next on four.

What was the biggest win in Group B on Matchday 1?

Legacy FFA's 7-1 win over NBFC was the biggest margin in Group B, a six-goal difference. Passion FC's 4-3 win over Veltrix United was the highest-scoring match.

How many goals were scored in Group B on Matchday 1?

42 goals were scored across the eight Group B matches, an average of 5.25 goals per game.

Did any Group B team keep a clean sheet?

Yes. First Touch FC beat Veltrix United 3-0, the only clean sheet recorded in Group B on Matchday 1.
