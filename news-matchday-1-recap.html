Baller League Matchday 1 Recap: Results, Scorers And Group A Standings

Knuckleball Editorial · 9 November 2026

Five Group A matches, 28 goals and a new early leader. Here is the full Baller League Matchday 1 recap: every supplied scoreline, every scorer and the Group A table after the opening round in Bengaluru.

Group A of The Baller League Season 2 produced five results, 28 goals and an early table that already has a clear leader. United 4th Phase ended Matchday 1 with six points from two games, while NEXUS FC, WLWG FC and Bangalore Grafters FC all sit within three points of the summit.

All five matches were played on Sunday 1 November at Play365 Vidyaranyapura in Bengaluru. Below is the full recap: every supplied scoreline, every supplied scorer, the Group A standings and the early top-scorers list.

NEXUS FC 3-0 GRVTY FC: result awarded after forfeit

Official result: NEXUS FC 3-0 GRVTY FC. NEXUS FC were awarded the win after the fixture was recorded as a walkover following an eligibility issue. Chethan scored twice in the match. The on-field score before the walkover adjustment was 2-1, so the official 3-0 result is the one that counts in the Group A table.

United 4th Phase 2-1 Bangalore Grafters FC

United 4th Phase opened their campaign with a 2-1 win. Hari and Subbaiah scored for United, while Aju replied for Bangalore Grafters. The result gave United the early advantage in the group.

WLWG FC 4-2 Walkalone FC: from 2-0 down to 4-2 up

Walkalone led 2-0 through Shree and Rohan, but WLWG responded with four unanswered goals. Daniel scored twice, while Rishabh and Puneeth also found the net. The comeback gave WLWG three points from their opening two games and became the standout result of the round.

Bangalore Grafters FC 10-2 Walkalone FC

Bangalore Grafters produced the biggest scoreline of Group A so far. Adnan scored four, Fazil three, and Abhinav, Aju and Mathai added one each. Shree scored both of Walkalone's goals. The win moved Grafters to second on goal difference.

United 4th Phase 3-1 WLWG FC

United 4th Phase made it two wins from two with a 3-1 victory over WLWG. AD scored twice, Hakee Zyech added one, and Uthappa scored WLWG's consolation. United ended the matchday top with six points.

Group A standings after Matchday 1

  • United 4th Phase: 6 pts (P2, GD +3)
  • Bangalore Grafters FC: 3 pts (P2, GD +7)
  • NEXUS FC: 3 pts (P1, GD +3)
  • WLWG FC: 3 pts (P2, GD 0)
  • GRVTY FC: 0 pts (P1, GD -3)
  • Walkalone FC: 0 pts (P2, GD -10)
  • First Draft FC: 0 pts (P0, fixtures postponed)
  • Velocity FC: 0 pts (P0, fixtures postponed)

Top scorers after Matchday 1

  • Fazil (Bangalore Grafters FC): 3 goals
  • Chethan (NEXUS FC): 2 goals
  • Daniel (WLWG FC): 2 goals
