How The Baller League Works: Season 2 Format, Points And Tiebreakers Explained

Knuckleball Editorial · 12 November 2026

A plain-English guide to Season 02: the two groups, the points system, how goal difference decides ties, how scorers are counted and what is still to be confirmed.

Short answer: The Baller League is a Bengaluru-based amateur football competition organised by Knuckleball. Season 02 is played at Play365 Vidyaranyapura with 16 teams split into Group A and Group B, three points for a win, and goal difference separating teams level on points.

What is the Baller League?

The Baller League is Knuckleball's flagship competitive football league for amateur players in Bengaluru. Season 02 (branded S02 / 026) runs a group stage at Play365 Vidyaranyapura, with results, scorer lines and tables published match by match on this site.

Knuckleball Global Pvt Ltd is the parent organisation behind the league, alongside the Baller Club community and learning initiative. Highlights and full-match replays from Season 02 are collected on the Media page.

Which teams play in Season 02?

Season 02 is split into two groups of eight. Group A: United 4th Phase, Bangalore Grafters FC, NEXUS FC, WLWG FC, GRVTY FC, Walkalone FC, First Draft FC and Velocity FC. Group B: Legacy FFA, Passion FC, First Touch FC, United 5's, Crescents FC, NBFC, Veltrix United and Falcons FC.

Every squad and shirt number is listed on the Players page, and each player has a profile with appearances, goals and honours.

How are points awarded?

The Baller League uses the standard football points system: three points for a win, one for a draw and none for a defeat. The published tables reflect it: two wins from two matches puts Legacy FFA and Passion FC on six points, and a single win puts First Touch FC, United 5's, Crescents FC and NBFC on three.

How are teams separated when they are level on points?

Goal difference is the tiebreak shown in the published tables. In Group A, Bangalore Grafters FC (+7), NEXUS FC (+3) and WLWG FC (0) are all on three points and are ordered by goal difference. In Group B the same rule puts Legacy FFA (+10) above Passion FC (+4) at the top.

Goal difference is calculated as goals scored minus goals conceded across the group matches a team has played.

How is a matchday structured?

Matches are played across both groups at a single venue, Play365 Vidyaranyapura in Bengaluru. Group A's opening round covered five fixtures; Group B's covered eight. Each team has played two group matches except NEXUS FC and GRVTY FC, who have played one, and First Draft FC and Velocity FC, whose Matchday 1 fixtures were postponed.

The group stage runs across four matchdays: Sunday 1 November, Sunday 15 November, Sunday 22 November and Sunday 29 November 2026. Group A plays the afternoon or early-evening block and Group B the other, with fixtures split across Turf 1 and Turf 2.

How are the top scorers worked out?

Every goal in a match is credited to the scoring player in the top-scorers table, except own goals, which count for the result but not for an individual tally. After Matchday 1 the chart is led by Darshan of Legacy FFA on eight goals, ahead of Adnan and Dilsher on four.

The live table is on the Competition page. Match reports and recaps are collected on the News page.

How do players and teams join?

Registration and squad enquiries run through the league's "Join the League" call to action, which links to the Players page. Entry requirements, fees and the Season 03 calendar are not published on this site; those details come from Knuckleball directly.

What is not yet confirmed?

The knockout or final-round format and new dates for three postponed Group A fixtures are still to be published. This page describes only what the league has supplied so far.
